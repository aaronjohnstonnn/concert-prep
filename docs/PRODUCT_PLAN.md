# Product Plan

## Problem

People attending concerts often want to familarise themselves with the performing artists beforehand, but they need to search across multiple artists, albums and streaming platforms.

## Target User

Someone who has tickets to an upcoming concert and wants to discover or familarise themselves with songs by the artists performing.

## Core User Journey

1. The user searches by artist or upcoming concert.
2. The app shows the relevant artist and concert information.
3. The app displays songs from the artist or concert lineup.
4. Songs are ranked so the user knows which ones to prioritise.
5. The user can open a song in Spotify or Apple Music.

## MVP

- Search for an artist by name.
- See the artist’s information.
- View a list of the artist’s songs.
- See songs sorted from most to least popular.
- Open a song in Spotify.
- See clear loading, empty, and error states.

## Success measures

- A user can find an artist and receive useful song recommendations in under 30 seconds.
- The app handles searches with no results without breaking.
- The app is publicly accessible through a deployed URL.
- The recommendation list includes songs from every major artist in a multi-artist concert lineup.

## Future Additions

- Playlist Generation
- User Accounts
- AI generated reccomendations
- Setlist-based reccomendations
- AI explanations for why each song is selected
